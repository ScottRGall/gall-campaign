Staff of the Greyvein Hearth

Staff, rare (requires attunement by a bard, cleric, or druid)

Greyvein's town healer kept this hearth-crook on the top shelf for mine disasters—the iron shod with silver banding, head carved like a curled flame over stone.

Visual (read aloud)

The staff is taller than a walking stick and heavier than it looks—black iron worn smooth where countless hands have gripped it. Silver bands ring the shaft in three places, dull from use, not polish. The head is a hearth-crook: iron bent into a gentle hook, and at the curve a carving like a flame curled over stone—miner-healer work, practical, not pretty. When you take it up, it is warm, as if it had been resting near coals. You catch coal smoke and crushed yarrow on the metal.

Properties

  • You have a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls while you hold this staff.
  • The staff has 10 charges. While holding it, you can expend charges to cast:
    • Cure wounds (1 charge per spell level, maximum 5 charges per cast)
    • Lesser restoration (2 charges)
    • Mass cure wounds (5 charges)
  • The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn.
  • If the staff's last charge is expended, roll a d20; on a 1, the staff retains its +1 bonus but loses all other properties.

How the party earns it

Offered by Mayor Dorrim and Brynn Coalwort if the party slays the purple worm beneath Greyvein (Session 6 contract).

Flavor Notes

  • Warm to the touch near wounded miners; faint smell of coal smoke and crushed yarrow.
  • Brynn says it "pulls pain toward the ground"—old clan miner-healer talk, not precise arcana.
